Our Story

Fire, Smoke
& Flavour

Grill Lounge was started at Uphill, Malappuram — near the Passport Office and close to Kottakkunnu — with one goal: bring real variety to Shawaya and Alfaham, rather than the usual one or two options.

Our signature Koila Shawaya is cooked over real charcoal, using a cooking machine we specially designed and developed ourselves. No shortcuts, no gas flame — just fire and smoke doing the work.

Alongside our grill specialities we also serve rice, Chinese dishes and other restaurant favourites, all built around one idea: good food, real variety, unique flavour.
